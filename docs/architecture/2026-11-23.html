FOREST: Coordinating Tree-based HBF, RRAM, and HBM for Efficient Speculative LLM Inference

arXiv 提交 2026-11-20 cs.AR

Hao Ding, Zheng Shi, Dongxue Zhao, Huiying Bian 等 10 人

中文摘要机器翻译

光谱解码(SD)通过将轻量级的起草与平行的目标模型校验相结合,加速了大型语言模型(LLM)推理. 然而,其各阶段都提出了不同的资源需求:目标核查需要高通量地访问大权重集,顺序起草在紧迫性很强的制约下执行紧凑模型,以及长效关注过程不断增长的密钥值(KV)缓存. 常规GPU-centricSD系统通过共享HBM-centric内存和通用计算为这些工作负载服务,在容量,带宽,和计算方面造成不匹配. 这些不匹配限制了可部署的模型-文本配置,并限制了带宽和耐久性敏感的操作的效率. 我们提议FOREST,一个异构内存中心架构协调HBF,3D-sacked RRAM,以及HBM内存计算树,以高效SD. 它的特点是:(1)计算对齐的3DRRAM,将超宽,低频读取与时钟脱节的接口相结合,并重叠SRAM-DCIM重量重填进行耐久性敏感的起草;(2)跨树数字共设计,移动块积分的Q/K/V预处理到HBF和RRAM根板,并使得块浮点(BFP)与HBM树的管道软轴减量相结合;以及(3)HBF-awareSD设计-空间探索,揭示带宽压缩供给和模型尺度如何决定性能,能量,以及成本权衡. 【该句原文】Evaluated across six SD configurations spanning 32B-405B target models, FOREST achieves up to 5.5x end-to-end speedup, 11.46x higher decode throughput, and 16.5x higher decode energy efficiency over NVIDIA A800 GPU-based SD baselines. 根据所有制的模型化总成本,FOREST提供最高6.73x更高的代号生成成本效率.

查看英文原文摘要

Speculative decoding (SD) accelerates large language model (LLM) inference by combining lightweight drafting with parallel target-model verification. However, its stages impose distinct resource demands: target verification requires high-throughput access to large weight sets, sequential drafting executes a compact model under tight latency constraints, and long-context attention processes growing key-value (KV) caches. Conventional GPU-centric SD systems serve these workloads through shared HBM-centric memory and general-purpose compute, creating mismatches in capacity, bandwidth, and computation. These mismatches constrain deployable model-context configurations and limit the efficiency of bandwidth-bound and latency-sensitive operations. We propose FOREST, a heterogeneous memory-centric architecture coordinating HBF, 3D-stacked RRAM, and HBM memory-compute trees for efficient SD. It features: (1) compute-aligned 3D RRAM, combining ultra-wide, low-frequency reads with clock-decoupled interfaces and overlapped SRAM-DCIM weight refills for latency-sensitive drafting; (2) cross-tree numerical co-design, moving block-scaled Q/K/V preprocessing to HBF and RRAM root plates and enabling block-floating-point (BFP) attention with pipelined softmax reduction in the HBM tree; and (3) HBF-aware SD design-space exploration, revealing how bandwidth-compute provisioning and model scale determine performance, energy, and cost trade-offs. Evaluated across six SD configurations spanning 32B-405B target models, FOREST achieves up to 5.5x end-to-end speedup, 11.46x higher decode throughput, and 16.5x higher decode energy efficiency over NVIDIA A800 GPU-based SD baselines. Under a modeled total cost of ownership, FOREST delivers up to 6.73x higher token-generation cost efficiency.

Exploring the Potential of Ultra-Low-Bit Inference on LLM Accelerators

arXiv 提交 2026-11-20 cs.AR

Hao Wang, Yang Shi, Jiageng Shi, Qiuran Li 等 5 人

中文摘要机器翻译

矢量量化(VQ)通过编码几组值作为紧凑的编码本索引,可以超低位压缩大语言模型(LLMs). 然而,重建这些数值进行计算需要频繁的代码本检查,这与常规的LLM加速器的数据流相冲突,限制了VQ压缩的性能收益. 因此,先前的工作采用了VQ-specific加速器设计,这种设计要么造成大量额外的硬件间接费用,要么只支持范围有限的推理设想方案。 在这项工作中,我们争论说,现有的基于系统-阵列的LLM加速器已经拥有高效VQ推理的基本硬件资源,但缺乏有效利用这些硬件的机制. 为了弥补这一差距,我们提出了VQACC,即一种建筑改进,联合重新设计现有的操作缓冲器的存取逻辑和数据映射,将其存储和端口资源重新用于代码簿的搜索,无缝地将脱量化整合到正常的操作地址数据流中. 评价显示,VQACC比EVA基线实现2.63×加速,额外的硬件仅占据计算阵列面积的0.17%.

查看英文原文摘要

Vector quantization (VQ) enables ultra-low-bit compression of large language models (LLMs) by encoding groups of values as compact codebook indexes. However, reconstructing these values for computation requires frequent codebook lookups, which conflict with the dataflows of conventional LLM accelerators and limit the performance gains from VQ compression. Consequently, prior work has resorted to VQ-specific accelerator designs, which either incur substantial additional hardware overhead or support only a limited range of inference scenarios. In this work, we argue that existing systolic-array-based LLM accelerators already possess the essential hardware resources for efficient VQ inference, but lack the mechanisms to effectively exploit them. To bridge this gap, we present VQACC, an architectural enhancement that jointly redesigns the existing operand buffer's access logic and data mapping to reuse its storage and port resources for codebook lookups, seamlessly integrating dequantization into the normal operand-addressing dataflow. Evaluation shows that VQACC achieves a 2.63$\times$ speedup over the EVA baseline, with additional hardware occupying only 0.17\% of the compute array's area.

评价显示,VQACC比EVA基线实现2.63×加速,额外的硬件仅占据计算阵列面积的0.17%.
创新点
1. 为了弥补这一差距,我们提出了VQACC,即一种建筑改进,联合重新设计现有的操作缓冲器的存取逻辑和数据映射,将其存储和端口资源重新用于代码簿的搜索,无缝地将脱量化整合到正常的操作地址数据流中.

查看英文原文摘要

The emergence of inverse-designed, pixelated passives for microwave and millimeter-wave applications has been a major development in the field of RFIC design. These devices, which appear to be QR-code-like in structure, exist in a generalizable, high-dimensional design space that includes conventional passive topologies. However, these structures are naturally unintuitive for designers, and failures or deviations in performance are difficult to debug. In this work, we ask whether a frontier language model can understand, predict, and edit the responses and layout structure of pixelated passives. The agent, run with all tools disabled, is first asked to read layouts on 18x18 and 27x27 tile grids and, in a single reply, predict the input impedance and loss of the structure from 20-100 GHz. On eight GA-synthesized layouts and five random fills, the predicted Smith-chart locus follows full-wave simulation results closely, with a systematic shift that places features about 16% lower in frequency than EM. On multipath loop structures it identifies notches successfully but shows a smaller frequency bias, about half that of an ideal transmission-line model that ignores bends. The agent is then asked to debug seven designs that fail their specification, and given a budget of five EM simulations and at most twelve changed tiles, it is able to improve all seven and bring five designs within specification. These experiments suggest language models can serve as an interpretability and debugging tool for traditionally non-intuitive inverse-designed passives.

We describe an analog-in-memory-compute macro hybrid-bonding a 14nm logic die onto a 3D NAND Flash memory wafer, achieving weight density of 4.8M weights/mm$^2$. Cruella: A Unified Microscaling and Floating-Point Dot-Product Unit for LLM Inference on FPGAs

arXiv 提交 2026-11-20 cs.AR

Jinho Yang, Taehyeong Kim, Mincheol Cha, Hyeoncheol Kim 等 15 人

中文摘要机器翻译

加速器在本地执行的数字格式是固定在编造上的,然而大型语言模型(LLM)检查站则在2023年的微缩缩格式(MX)中以MXFP4的重量发布. FPGAs通过位流采用新格式. 然而,据我们所知,没有部署FPGALLM加速器与BF16产品一起执行MXFP4重量。 我们描述的是MXFP4检查站的解码,其层层在MXFP4xBF16产品和BF16xBF16引起注意的产品和BF16层之间交替。 带有BF16KV缓存,BF16xBF16产品占用了56%-77%的数据路径时间,在此期间一个单独的MXFP4单元闲置. 我们提出Cruella,其统一的点产品单位(UDPU)在一种DSP阵列上执行两种产品类型和减少管道。 首先,每组预对齐使这个管道保持BF16宽度,并最多改变0.7%的复杂度. 其次,添加剂前包装将两个签有MXFP4×BF16的产品放在一个DSP48E2乘数中,并精确校正. 在 Alveo U55C 上,UDPU 需要减少23%的DSP切片和36%的LUTs,比分离的MXFP4和BF16单位的均匀吞吐量少. 在这个板子上,MXFP4重量以1.15×-1.87×超过BF16重量加速解码,一个NVIDIA A40GPU解码速度较快,但每个符牌花费0.92×-2.01×的板子能量.

查看英文原文摘要

The number formats that accelerators execute natively are fixed at fabrication, yet large language model (LLM) checkpoints are released with MXFP4 weights in the Microscaling (MX) formats standardized in 2023. FPGAs adopt new formats through bitstreams. However, to the best of our knowledge, no deployed FPGA LLM accelerator executes MXFP4 weights alongside BF16 products. We characterize the decoding of MXFP4 checkpoints, whose layers alternate between MXFP4$\times$BF16 products and BF16$\times$BF16 products of attention and BF16 layers. With a BF16 KV cache, BF16$\times$BF16 products take 56%-77% of the datapath time, during which a separate MXFP4 unit idles. We present Cruella, whose unified dot-product unit (UDPU) executes both product types on one DSP array and reduction pipeline. First, per-group pre-alignment keeps this pipeline at the BF16 width and changes perplexity by at most 0.7%. Second, pre-adder packing places two signed MXFP4$\times$BF16 products in one DSP48E2 multiplier with an exact correction. On an Alveo U55C, the UDPU needs 23% fewer DSP slices and 36% fewer LUTs than separate MXFP4 and BF16 units of equal throughput. On this board, MXFP4 weights speed up decoding by 1.15$\times$-1.87$\times$ over BF16 weights, and an NVIDIA A40 GPU decodes faster but spends 0.92$\times$-2.01$\times$ the board's energy per token.

核心结论
在这个板子上,MXFP4重量以1.15×-1.87×超过BF16重量加速解码,一个NVIDIA A40GPU解码速度较快,但每个符牌花费0.92×-2.01×的板子能量.

GRADPlace: Geometry-Based Routing-Augmented Differentiable Placement for Routability Optimization

arXiv 提交 2026-11-20 cs.AR

Xurong Shen, Xueyan Zhao, Zhipeng Huang, Chaoyu Xing 等 5 人

中文摘要机器翻译

可调节性驱动的放置需要梯度,以显示细胞运动如何重新分配路由需求. 细胞膨胀只能间接利用路由反馈,不同的方法依赖于黑箱拥堵预测器,或者依赖于在路由更新之间冻结的虚拟细胞拥堵场. 我们提出GRADPlace,表示概率加权的候选路线,作为可移动的横向和纵向部分。 运行反馈设置了它们的概率,而它们的几何和预期需求则随细胞坐标而演变. 一个静电的路由目标通过这种几何向连接的细胞传播梯度,补充细胞膨胀. 在ISPD2015年基准上,我们复制的Xplace-route和公布的DCGP结果平均有3.37x和2.56x与GRADPlace一样多的设计-规则违规,通过和可比较的路由线长.

查看英文原文摘要

Routability-driven placement needs gradients that show how cell movement redistributes routing demand. Cell inflation exploits routing feedback only indirectly, and differentiable methods rely on black-box congestion predictors or on virtual-cell congestion fields frozen between routing updates. We present GRADPlace, which represents probability-weighted candidate routes as movable horizontal and vertical segments. Routing feedback sets their probabilities, while their geometry and expected demand evolve with cell coordinates. An electrostatic routing objective propagates gradients through this geometry to connected cells, complementing cell inflation. On the ISPD 2015 benchmarks, our reproduced Xplace-route and published DCGP results have on average 3.37x and 2.56x as many design-rule violations as GRADPlace, with more vias and comparable routed wirelength.

Stratum: A Hardware-Software Co-Design Framework for SNN Inference on RISC-V

arXiv 提交 2026-11-20 cs.AR

Wajahat Riaz, Muhammad Adeel Pasha, Shahid Masud, Muhammad Ali Siddiqi

中文摘要机器翻译

RISC-V处理器的高效脉冲神经网络(SNN)推理需要追踪执行成本回溯到生成它们的编译器和硬件决定. 然而,培训环境、编译器和循环模拟器通常作为独特的工具运作,使这种归属变得困难。 本文提出Stratum,一个框架将一个snnTorch训练的SNN,一个MLIR-based编译器堆栈,以及一个周期级的基于gem5的RISC-V模型集成到一个统一的工作流程中. 查看英文原文摘要

Efficient spiking neural network (SNN) inference on RISC-V processors requires tracing implementation costs back to the compiler and hardware decisions that produce them. Training environments, compilers, and cycle-level simulators, however, typically operate as distinct tools, making this attribution difficult. This paper presents Stratum, a framework that integrates an snnTorch-trained SNN, an MLIR-based compiler stack, and a cycle-level gem5-based RISC-V model into a unified workflow. Stratum exposes two optimization levers, software decisions in the generated code and hardware configuration choices in the simulated core, and uses stage-level cycle measurements to identify performance bottlenecks. We demonstrate Stratum on a two-layer N-MNIST network with three controlled comparisons: (i)~dense vs.\ spike-gated lowering of \texttt{snn.linear}, (ii)~output-outer vs.\ spike-major weight layout with spike-gating enabled in both binaries, and (iii)~L2 cache capacity with both layouts. Our spike-gated lowering reduces simulated inference time by $64\times$ (453.2\,ms to 7.04\,ms), and our spike-major layout cuts it by a further 37.2\% (to 4.42\,ms) at 4\,MiB L2, with the predicted class unchanged. The layout speedup grows from $1.59\times$ to $1.88\times$ when L2 shrinks to 1\,MiB. These results show that Stratum enables rapid early evaluation of design choices at both the software level (compiler stack) and the hardware level (cycle-level gem5 simulation).

Mind the Delta: Early Pruning of RTL Variants for Agentic Hardware Optimization

arXiv 提交 2026-11-20 cs.AR

Autonomous coding agents powered by large language models (LLMs) now write diverse microarchitectural variants of existing register-transfer level (RTL) designs at high throughput, shifting the primary bottleneck of agentic RTL optimization onto logic synthesis. Commercial toolchains take hours per variant, creating an acute compute asymmetry between proposing and evaluating variants. Existing fast evaluators falter in this regime. Subtle RTL edits trigger nonlinear synthesis shifts that learned predictors and coding agents cannot resolve, while synthesis proxies at reduced effort either alter the optimization trajectory or retain most of its runtime. Selecting a variant, however, does not require regressing absolute quality of results across designs, only identifying when a candidate cannot surpass the best completed feasible implementation. Based on the observation that synthesis trajectories share strong design-specific bias with their base design, we propose DiffScope, a training-free anytime selection framework that races full synthesis runs in parallel. By pairing live area telemetry with the already synthesized base trajectory, DiffScope cancels early mapping inflation, reducing residual variance during initial mapping by 73.6X, and stops uncompetitive runs via a lower bound calibrated on other design lineages. Across 43 design families from small blocks to processors with millions of cells, DiffScope uniquely recovers the optimum of exhaustive synthesis in every family, cuts total evaluation work by 44.9% (1.81X), and reaches 2.18X on L-tier designs.

核心结论
将候选综合过程与同源基准设计作差，可提前淘汰低潜力变体。DiffScope 在 43 个设计族中保留了穷举综合的最优结果，并将总评估工作量减少 44.9%。
创新点
用已综合基准设计的轨迹消除同源变体在早期映射中的共有面积偏差，并以跨设计校准的下界决定何时停止完整综合任务。

中文摘要

近似最近邻搜索的向量扫描受存储带宽限制，适合用存内计算（PIM）加速。现有方案往往把面向 CPU 的距离计算和前 k 名选择直接移植到 PIM，给轻量、彼此隔离的核心带来较高的算术和状态开销。PIVOT 对向量进行二值量化，通过 PIM 原生位运算直接估算距离，无需查找表，也降低了本地存储需求。它用候选保留量模型导出保守阈值，分别指导 CPU 上的簇筛选和 PIM 上的距离下界过滤，由此减少回传数据并省去各 PIM 核心的前 k 名状态。CPU 负责查询级协调和重排序，PIM 执行数据并行过滤。系统为配置生成专用 PIM 内核，并跨查询批次重叠 CPU 准备与重排序和 PIM 过滤。真实 UPMEM 硬件上的实验表明，PIVOT 剔除了被探查向量的 99.3%–99.7%，查询吞吐最高分别达到 CPU 基线的 18.8 倍和以往 PIM 基线的 10.2 倍。其召回率高于已有 PIM 系统，在高维嵌入上也保持有效。

查看英文原文摘要

Approximate nearest neighbor search (ANNS) is a key primitive in modern data-intensive systems, and its memory-bound vector-scanning stage makes processing-in-memory (PIM) a promising acceleration platform. Existing PIM-based ANNS systems largely transplant CPU-oriented distance evaluation and top-$k$ selection onto PIM, introducing arithmetic- and state-heavy operations that poorly match lightweight, isolated PIM cores. To address these mismatches, we present PIVOT, a PIM-native vector search system with cooperative filtering, which thoroughly rethinks how ANNS should be executed on commodity PIM. First, PIVOT recasts PIM-side distance evaluation around binary quantization to exploit PIM-native bitwise operations, enabling LUT-free evaluation with low arithmetic cost and a small local-memory footprint. Second, PIVOT redesigns PIM-side candidate selection around threshold-guided cooperative filtering, using a survivor-mass model to derive a conservative threshold for CPU-side cluster filtering and PIM-side lower-bound filtering, thereby reducing output traffic and eliminating per-core top-$k$ state. PIVOT organizes these stages into a cooperative workflow that keeps query-global coordination and reranking on the CPU while assigning memory-intensive, data-parallel filtering to PIM. To efficiently realize this workflow and sustain high utilization across devices, PIVOT further generates configuration-specialized PIM kernels and pipelines CPU-side preparation and reranking with PIM-side filtering across query batches. Evaluations on real UPMEM hardware show that PIVOT filters out 99.3-99.7% of probed vectors and achieves up to $18.8\times$ and $10.2\times$ the QPS of CPU and prior PIM baselines, respectively. PIVOT also achieves higher recall than existing PIM systems and remains effective on high-dimensional embeddings.

核心结论
PIM 端用位运算和阈值过滤承担大规模扫描，CPU 保留全局排序职责。真实 UPMEM 实验中，PIVOT 剔除了超过 99% 的被探查向量，相比以往 PIM 基线实现最高 10.2 倍的查询吞吐，同时提高召回率。
创新点
用二值量化实现适合 PIM 的无查找表距离估计，再以候选保留量模型统一指导 CPU 簇筛选与 PIM 距离下界过滤，省去每个核心的前 k 名状态。

Acceleration of Sparse and Irregular Workloads through Energy-Efficient Stream Extensions

Paul Scheffler

中文摘要

稀疏神经网络、多物理场仿真和图分析等工作负载缺少规则计算常见的局部性、数据复用及稳定控制流，传统 CPU 和 GPU 的功能单元利用率可能低于 10%。本论文研究低能耗硬件流扩展，通过将数据搬运与计算解耦，在通用系统中加速稀疏和不规则工作负载，同时维持规则负载性能。作者设计了与处理核心紧耦合的流扩展，支持仿射访存流、间接访存流，以及稀疏张量索引的交集与并集。该扩展被集成到低能耗 RISC-V 计算集群，用于通用稀疏线性代数、模板计算、脉冲神经网络推理和模型预测控制。作者还在采用 12 nm FinFET 工艺制造的 432 核 2.5D RISC-V 系统中验证了不规则负载下的计算利用率与能效，并将芯片级互连扩展为支持端到端的不规则访存流，改善性能、能效与总线利用率。

查看英文原文摘要

The slowdown of Moore's law and Dennard scaling has motivated computer architects to uphold performance scaling through architectural specialization. So far, most efforts have focused on highly regular workloads, which exhibit good data locality, ample reuse, and a data-independent execution flow. As this specialization hits diminishing returns, attention shifts towards sparse and irregular workloads, which are essential to applications like sparse neural networks, multiphysics simulations, and graph analytics. However, they lack the convenient properties of highly regular compute that modern architectures are optimized for, resulting in very low (<10%) functional unit utilization on traditional CPUs and GPUs. The most promising approaches to their general hardware acceleration leverage memory streams, which decouple data movement from execution. In this thesis, we explore energy-efficient hardware stream extensions that significantly accelerate sparse and irregular workloads in general-purpose systems without specializing to specific application domains or compromising on regular-workload performance. We design a flexible, core-coupled stream extension supporting affine and indirect streams as well as intersection and union of sparse tensors. We integrate it into an energy-efficient RISC-V compute cluster, achieving significant performance and energy efficiency benefits on general sparse linear algebra, and further use it to accelerate stencil computations, spiking neural network inference, and model-predictive control. We evaluate it at scale in a 432-core 2.5D RISC-V system fabricated in 12nm FinFET, achieving leading compute utilization and energy efficiency on sparse and irregular workloads while remaining competitive on regular workloads. Finally, we extend chip-level interconnects with end-to-end irregular streams, improving performance, energy efficiency, and bus utilization.

核心结论
将不规则数据搬运交给通用流扩展，可在同一硬件上支持多种稀疏负载并改善执行单元利用率。论文在 432 核 2.5D RISC-V 实体系统验证了这一思路，并将其延伸至片上互连。
创新点
提出支持仿射与间接访存、稀疏索引交并的核旁流扩展，再把端到端不规则流支持融入芯片级互连。
