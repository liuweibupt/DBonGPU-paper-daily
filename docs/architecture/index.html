OpenWoud: Vendor-Unlocked Scalable Adaptive Random Forest Accelerator

arXiv 提交 2026-11-24 cs.AR cs.LG

Ewout van Nimwegen, Elijah Seth Cishugi, Hendrik Folmer, Marco Ottavi 等 5 人

中文摘要机器翻译

在线机器学习(ML)可以对不断发展的数据流进行实时决策. 这些动态模型不断学习适应新概念,因为以前的模型变得无效,但其性能往往受到吞吐量的限制. 适应性随机森林(ARF)是这种设置的最先进的模型,它自然会暴露平行性,因此非常适合硬件加速. 然而,先前的外地可编程门阵列(FPGA)基于供应商的加速器是锁定的,依赖于资源饥饿Floating-Point(FP)算术,限制了它们的可缩放性和可移植性. 我们提出OpenWoud,这是FPGAs的供应商不受限制的ARF框架。 我们的设计用一个灵活的FiXed-Point(FxP)算术库取代FP操作员,并引入完全透明、手动安排的数据流,用于培训和漂移检测。 这使得各平台之间时间/地区权衡得当和行为可预测成为可能。 对合成和真实数据集的综合评价显示,OpenWoud保持或提高精度(最高为+12.89%),而之前的FPGA加速器则将资源利用率降低一半. 与GPU(0.5 - 1.5x加速)和FPGA基线(0.2 - 0.5x)相比,观察到了竞争性能,尽管后者是基于理想化的模拟。 关键是,OpenWoud维持真实的逐样样本推理,与需要批量插入的GPUs不同.

查看英文原文摘要

Online Machine Learning (ML) enables real-time decision-making on evolving data streams. These dynamic models learn continuously to adapt to new concepts, as previous ones become invalid, but their performance is often limited by throughput. The Adaptive Random Forest (ARF) is a state-of-the-art model for this setting, which naturally exposes parallelism and is therefore well-suited for hardware acceleration. However, prior Field Programmable Gate Array (FPGA)-based accelerators are vendor-locked and rely on resource-hungry Floating-Point (FP) arithmetics, limiting their scalability and portability. We present OpenWoud, a vendor-unlocked ARF framework for FPGAs. Our design replaces FP operators with a flexible FiXed-Point (FxP) arithmetic library and introduces fully transparent, manually scheduled dataflows for training and drift detection. This enables fine-grained time/area trade-offs and predictable behavior across platforms. A comprehensive evaluation on both synthetic and real datasets shows that OpenWoud maintains or increases accuracy (up to +12.89%), compared to a prior FPGA accelerator, while reducing the resource utilization by half. Competitive performance is observed compared to GPU (0.5 - 1.5x speed-up) and an FPGA baseline (0.2 - 0.5x), despite the latter being based on idealized simulation. Crucially, OpenWoud sustains true sample-by-sample inference, unlike GPUs that require batch insertion.

核心结论
对合成和真实数据集的综合评价显示,OpenWoud保持或提高精度(最高为+12.89%),而之前的FPGA加速器则将资源利用率降低一半.
创新点
1. 我们提出OpenWoud,这是FPGAs的供应商不受限制的ARF框架。；2. 我们的设计用一个灵活的FiXed-Point(FxP)算术库取代FP操作员,并引入完全透明、手动安排的数据流,用于培训和漂移检测。

Power Side-Channel Evaluation using Multi-FPGA Systems

arXiv 提交 2026-11-24 cs.AR cs.CR

Yaswanth Tavva, Geng Luo, Trevor E. Carlson, Li-Shiuan Peh

中文摘要机器翻译

复杂 乱序 处理器在磁带解压前难以评价功率侧通道渗漏. 单一的FPGA往往缺乏足够的能力,而常规的CAD-based分析太慢,无法支持实际攻击所需的大量痕迹. Multi-FPGA 仿真提供了更大的能力,但现有的分割计划并没有安全意识,因此导致攻击性能不佳. 这部作品介绍了剪刀,一种安全意识multi-FPGA分区方法,用于对复杂乱序处理器进行前硅动力侧通道评价. 剪刀采用侧通道脆弱系数(SVF)分析,以识别与密钥相关的信号. 然后,它将强烈的密钥-cor相关信号限制在一个FPGA分区(测量)上,同时将其余的信号移动到另一个FPGAs(支持),提高侧通道信息的可观察性. 我们通过RTL模拟和在双U200 FPGA试验台上进行物理测量来评价剪刀,使用频率域CPA,并使用汉明-重量的渗漏模型. 查看英文原文摘要

Complex out-of-order processors are difficult to evaluate for power side-channel leakage before tapeout. A single FPGA often lacks sufficient capacity, whereas conventional CAD-based analysis is too slow to support the large numbers of traces required by practical attacks. Multi-FPGA emulation provides greater capacity, but existing partitioning schemes are not security-aware and thus results in poor attack performance. This work presents Scissors, a security-aware multi-FPGA partitioning approach for pre-silicon power side-channel evaluation of complex out-of-order processors. Scissors uses side-channel vulnerability factor (SVF) analysis to identify signals correlated with the secret key. It then keeps strongly key-correlated signals confined to one FPGA partition (measured) while moving rest of the signals to the other FPGAs (support), improving the observability of the side-channel information. We evaluate Scissors through RTL simulations and physical measurements on a two-U200 FPGA testbed, using frequency-domain CPA with a Hamming-weight leakage model. RTL ablation shows that only 68-71 high-SVF signals dominate key recovery process. For a single-core processor (SingleBOOM), Scissors recovers the key with 12,000 traces, compared with 49,000 for the unpartitioned design and 43,000 for TritonPart, while improving correct-key correlation by $2.83\times$. For a quad-core processor (QuadBOOM), which exceeds the capacity of the CW340 single-FPGA platform, Scissors reduces the required traces from 111,000 to 66,000 and improves correlation by $1.48\times$ over TritonPart. Scissors also reduces QuadBOOM RTL-to-bitstream generation from 61 h 39 min to 6 h 40 min, a $9.25\times$ speedup. These results show that security-aware partitioning improves leakage observability and attack performance while substantially reducing implementation time for complex processors against the baseline method.

查看英文原文摘要

Benchmarks such as VerilogEval have supported rapid advances in hardware description language (HDL) generation by large language models (LLMs). However, recent HDL-generation systems achieve pass rates above 90% on VerilogEval, leaving limited headroom for revealing further advances. To provide a challenging target for further progress on a practically important hardware-development task, we introduce CPU-Eval, a benchmark of 339 HDL-generation problems constructed from six CPUs. Drawn from practical CPU designs, including families fabricated in silicon, the problems span CPUs from small embedded cores to Linux-capable out-of-order cores and cover diverse CPU functions written in SystemVerilog, Chisel, and SpinalHDL. In each problem, an LLM performs the real CPU-development task of generating HDL. Across seven state-of-the-art LLMs, mean pass@1 is 45.14 percentage points lower on CPU-Eval than on VerilogEval v2 Spec-to-RTL, exposing capability differences obscured by VerilogEval's high pass rates. CPU-Eval reveals that pass@1 is lower for submodules with more source-code lines, which motivates fine-grained modular design for LLM-aided hardware generation. We also show that pass@1 is lower for CPUs written in less widely used HDLs, motivating designers to verify that an LLM is sufficiently proficient at generating code in the HDL they use before deployment.

Scaling Architectural Simulation to Giga Instructions Per Second

arXiv 提交 2026-11-24 cs.AR

Zhouyi Shen, Hao Tang, Ruike Liu, Jingwei Sun 等 5 人

中文摘要机器翻译

详细的建筑模拟成本制约了设计-空间探索,而模拟核心之间的平行化为单一核心和小型多核心目标提供了有限的宿主货币。 我们提出GIPSim,这是一个由指令驱动的模拟器,它从依赖性和资源限制中获得指令的时间安排,减少更新每个模拟周期的管道状态的间接费用。 它将同步制导的时间分解应用于记录的执行,暴露出目标核心计数之外的宿主平行性. GIPSim 以平行方式重放所形成的片段,并通过依赖图来汇总其持续时间,以估计总体执行时间,并使用保留微体系结构的状态,暖气,同步导引缓存驱离来重现环境重建. 这说明同步等待时不需要东道主工人遵守目标执行令. 我们使用QEMU在多核心x86-64,AArch64和RV64GC系统中的应用和操作系统执行痕迹来评价GIPSim. 【该句原文】GIPSim achieves an average single-node simulation throughput of 13.8 million instructions per second (MIPS) with one worker and 1{,}010 MIPS with 96 workers, peaking at 1{,}421 MIPS. 相对于宝石5,执行周期的相应平均绝对百分比差错分别为7.52%和7.43%.

查看英文原文摘要

The cost of detailed architectural simulation constrains design-space exploration, while parallelization across simulated cores offers limited host concurrency for single-core and small multicore targets. We present GIPSim, an instruction-driven simulator that derives instruction timing from dependencies and resource constraints, reducing the overhead of updating pipeline state every simulated cycle. It applies synchronization-guided temporal decomposition to recorded execution, exposing host parallelism beyond the target core count. GIPSim replays the resulting segments in parallel and aggregates their durations through a dependency graph to estimate overall execution time, with replay contexts reconstructed using retained microarchitectural state, warmup, and synchronization-guided cache eviction. This accounts for synchronization waits without requiring host workers to follow target execution order. We evaluate GIPSim using QEMU traces of application and operating system execution on multi-core x86-64, AArch64, and RV64GC systems. GIPSim achieves an average single-node simulation throughput of 13.8 million instructions per second (MIPS) with one worker and 1{,}010 MIPS with 96 workers, peaking at 1{,}421 MIPS. The corresponding mean absolute percentage errors in execution cycles relative to gem5 are 7.52\% and 7.43\%, respectively.

核心结论
【该句原文】GIPSim achieves an average single-node simulation throughput of 13.8 million instructions per second (MIPS) with one worker and 1{,}010 MIPS with 96 workers, peaking at 1{,}421 MIPS.
创新点
1. 我们提出GIPSim,这是一个由指令驱动的模拟器,它从依赖性和资源限制中获得指令的时间安排,减少更新每个模拟周期的管道状态的间接费用。；2. 它将同步制导的时间分解应用于记录的执行,暴露出目标核心计数之外的宿主平行性.

中文摘要机器翻译

Spiking神经网络(SNNs)为节能AI推理提供了一个有吸引力的范式,而它们部署在可重构硬件上则能够实现应用特定加速而不会牺牲模型的重编性. 然而,在FPGAs上实现这种潜力往往受到存储突触重量所需的巨大芯片内存的严重制约。 现有的压缩技术以重量精度或精度为代价减少了这种存储要求. 这部作品提出了混合位元级的权重存储方案,用于交换BRAM容量的FPGA逻辑资源,同时保留原始权重未修改. 选定重量位使用Look-Up表(LUTs)执行,其余位则存储在BRAM中,并在推理期间共同重建. 我们将此方法整合到空间数据流SNN架构中,并引入实用的位图速率法,在不修改权重的情况下在BRAM和LUT-based逻辑之间有效划分权重位. 实验性评价表明,BRAM的利用率下降了26%以上,LUT和登记使用情况略有增加,从而节省了大量总的电力。

查看英文原文摘要

Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) offer an attractive paradigm for energy-efficient AI inference, while their deployment on reconfigurable hardware enables application-specific acceleration without sacrificing model reprogrammability. However, realizing this potential on FPGAs is often severely constrained by the immense on-chip memory needed to store synaptic weights. Existing compression techniques reduce this storage requirement at the cost of weight precision or accuracy. This work presents a hybrid bit-level weight-storage scheme that trades BRAM capacity for FPGA logic resources while preserving the original weights unmodified. Selected weight bits are implemented using Look-Up Tables (LUTs), while the remaining bits are stored in BRAM and jointly reconstructed during inference. We integrate this approach into a spatial dataflow SNN architecture and introduce a practical bit-mapping heuristic that efficiently partitions weight bits between BRAM and LUT-based logic without modifying the weights. Experimental evaluation demonstrates more than 26% reduction in BRAM utilization with a modest increase in LUT and register usage, resulting in significant overall power savings.

Slow Control Systems (SCSs) are essential for the monitoring, configuration, and protection of distributed safety-critical systems in scientific, industrial, and medical applications. Although software-based SCS architectures offer flexibility and scalability, they suffer from software aging and non-deterministic latencies, which limit long-term reliability and timely reaction to critical errors, often requiring additional hardware interlock systems. This paper presents an FPGA-centric lightweight Slow Control Interlock System (SCIS) designed to achieve deterministic ultra-low-latency reaction times in long-running dependable systems. System parameters are acquired and fused at runtime in a Supervisory Monitor Unit (SMU), enabling real-time fault condition classification and prioritization through configurable priority rules and triggering kill-switch actions with microsecond-scale latency. This demonstrates the feasibility of our FPGA-centric architecture as a solution to the problem of combining ultra-low-latency interlock capabilities with the scalability of software-based systems.

Mayfly: Lifecycle-Aware High-Bandwidth Flash for Recursive Self-Improvement

arXiv 提交 2026-11-24 cs.AR

Chunlin Tian, Mingjia Fan, Feng Yu, Qingyun Zou 等 7 人

中文摘要机器翻译

递归自我改进(RSI)让LLM通过提出和评价跨任务的候选变体来改进其代理系统. 一代的会话会一起增长他们的KV缓存,并在完成时释放,从而形成一个突起,随后出现衰落. 在高通量时,即使是准备运行的会话的KV也超过了GPU的HBM. 卸载该 KV 以主机 DRAM 或 SSDs 添加容量,但在执行前留下准备好的会话等待 HBM 空格 。 High-Bandwidth Flash(HBF)可以就位为溢出服务,但过度读取长度解码步骤,重复写作会消耗闪存耐力. 我们的关键洞察力是,在读完KV后,应该跟随爆破及其下降:从HBF到位,而爆破的溢出HBM,只要每个步骤的带宽允许,再从HBM释放出来。 准备状态和停放状态之间的切换只决定一个块是否被读取,所以它的闪存副本仍然有效,一个会话结束,由管弦乐员宣布,决定它的私密时间KV. 我们提出Mayfly,生命周期制导HBF管理RSI.
